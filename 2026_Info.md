# 2026 FIFA World Cup — Teams & Bracket (reference for prediction/simulation)

Hosts: USA, Mexico, Canada. Tournament: 11 June – 19 July 2026. Format: 48 teams,
12 groups of 4, top 2 per group + 8 best 3rd-placed teams advance to a Round of 32,
then straight knockout (R16 → QF → SF → Final). 104 matches total.

## Modeling notes for Claude Code
- Host home advantage: Mexico, Canada, and USA play their matches at home —
  apply the Elo home-advantage term (+H) to them; treat all other World Cup
  matches as NEUTRAL venue unless a host is involved.
- The 8 best 3rd-placed teams are ranked by: points, then goal difference, then
  goals scored, then team conduct score (fair play/cards), then FIFA World Ranking.
  The mapping of
  which 3rd-place team fills which M74–M87 slot is a fixed FIFA lookup keyed on the
  exact set of qualifying groups — resolve the 3rd[…] slots only after all 72 group
  matches are simulated.
- Knockout matches cannot end in a draw: if level after 90', apply extra time then
  penalties. For Elo updates, a penalty-shootout result counts as a DRAW (W=0.5).
- Normalize team-name strings to match your results.csv (e.g. align "Türkiye"/
  "Turkey", "IR Iran"/"Iran", "Côte d'Ivoire"/"Ivory Coast", "Congo DR"/"DR Congo",
  "Czechia"/"Czech Republic", "Cabo Verde"/"Cape Verde") before joining to ratings.
